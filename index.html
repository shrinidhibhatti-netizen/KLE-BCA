About Our Institution

A Proud Legacy in Transformative IT & Computer Applications

The **Karnataka Lingayat Education (K.L.E.) Society** was established in 1916 with a monumental mission: providing selfless, progressive, and affordable education to all sections of society. Today, **KLE BCA** stands tall as a beacon of excellence in technology education, equipped with world-class faculty, autonomous curriculum freedom, and state-of-the-art incubation facilities.

Frequently Asked Questions (Admissions & BCA)

Candidates who have passed 10+2 / PUC II (Science, Commerce, or Arts) with an aggregate of 50% or more (45% for reserved categories) are eligible. Students who have completed a 3-Year Polytechnic Diploma recognized by the State Board of Technical Education can also apply directly.
Under NEP 2020, students can exit after 3 years with a standard Bachelor of Computer Applications degree, or proceed into the 4th Year to earn a BCA (Honours with Research). The 4th year includes advanced research papers, machine learning thesis, and enables direct eligibility into 1-Year Master's or direct Ph.D. programs globally.
KLE Society provides generous merit scholarships: >95% in 12th/PUC receives a 50% tuition waiver (Saptarshi Platinum), 88%-94.9% receives a 35% waiver (Gold), and 80%-87.9% receives a 20% waiver. You can calculate your exact scholarship using our interactive calculator above!
